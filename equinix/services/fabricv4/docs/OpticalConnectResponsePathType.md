# OpticalConnectResponsePathType

Level of resilience for the connection. <br> UNPROTECTED - a single fiber path with no node redundancy. <br> PROTECTED - two diverse fiber paths between single nodes at each IBX. <br> DUAL_DIVERSE - two diverse fiber paths terminating on redundant nodes at both IBXs. 

## Enum

* `DUAL_DIVERSE` (value: `'DUAL_DIVERSE'`)

* `PROTECTED` (value: `'PROTECTED'`)

* `UNPROTECTED` (value: `'UNPROTECTED'`)
